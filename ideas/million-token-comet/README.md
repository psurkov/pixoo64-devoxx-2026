# Million-Token Comet

**Status:** text concept only. No image generated.

## Core idea

Show the unusually long output trajectory announced for Gemini 4 Argon as a luminous comet of code orbiting an `Ar` core. The visible `1M` is the concept's short, readable hook.

## Proposed first static frame

- A dark 64×64 sky with one bright `Ar` crystal at the center.
- A curved trail of square light particles sweeps around the crystal. The larger particles form a clear `1M` near the upper right.
- The trail shifts from cool blue near the core to violet at its tail, giving the still frame a sense of motion.
- A few isolated pixel stars add depth; no small lines of code or explanatory text appear.

**Palette:** black, indigo, cobalt, violet, cyan, and white.

## Possible animation after approval

Particles leave the core, trace the long arc, briefly resolve into `1M`, then return. Keep the central `Ar` stable so the viewer can understand the image at any moment.

## Strength and risk

This could look striking as a GIF and refers to a specific, surprising Argon capability. Its meaning is less immediate than the bug or periodic tile concepts; the `1M` and `Ar` must stay legible at native size.
