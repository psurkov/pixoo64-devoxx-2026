# The Lights Stay On

**Status:** text concept only. No image generated.

## The human story

At dusk, people are visible in a few windows across a small town: someone reading, someone cooking, someone waving. A blue thread beneath the street quietly keeps the digital systems they rely on safe. Argon is presented as unseen work that lets ordinary life continue.

This is a visual metaphor for Argon's defensive cybersecurity work and its early use in protecting public infrastructure. It does not claim that Argon operates an electric grid.

## Proposed first static 64×64 frame

- A low, dark skyline spans the lower half of the square. Three homes have windows with distinct warm colors.
- Each window contains only one or two large silhouette pixels that suggest a person. One window shows two people together.
- Under the street, a single luminous cyan line curves past the homes. Near the center, a small repair point glows blue and white.
- A faint `Ar` shape is hidden in the curve of the blue line, as a detail for a second look.
- The sky stays mostly empty and dark, giving the few warm windows strong visual impact.

**Palette:** midnight blue, charcoal, warm yellow and orange windows, cyan and white for the hidden repair.

## Possible animation after approval

The blue line travels below the street; each window warms as it passes; a figure waves in the final window. The loop ends with the neighborhood quietly alive.

## Strength and risk

The scene is optimistic and readable from across a booth. Its connection to a named AI model is subtle, so the `Ar` detail and the accompanying repository story would need to make that link clear.
