## Verified context

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on September 30, 2026. It emphasizes long-horizon coding, enterprise work, and defensive cybersecurity. At the time of these notes, access is rolling out to trusted cyber defenders; Argon is the **subject** of the artwork, not the image generation model. The planned image generator is Nano Banana 2.1.
