# The Light on the Page

**Status:** text concept only. No image has been generated or approved.

## Core scene

A quiet, cozy, lo-fi study desk seen from almost directly above. A person sits at the bottom edge, studying an open notebook. A small incandescent desk lamp turns on. Its warm pool of light lands on the pages, where rows of handwritten-looking marks gradually appear. The simple change from a dark, empty page to a bright, full one carries the story.

Argon is used inside incandescent bulbs to protect the filament. The lamp is a visual metaphor for Gemini 4 Argon helping someone learn; the glowing filament makes the light, not the gas. The model name can be explained alongside the artwork rather than squeezed into the pixels.

## Proposed first static 64×64 frame

- Fixed, nearly overhead composition. The open notebook fills the center and right side; the lamp sits near the upper left; the student's shoulders and one hand enter from the bottom.
- The lamp is already on. A clear amber circle of light makes the notebook the brightest, largest shape. The surrounding desk stays dark and calm.
- About half the notebook is filled with short, evenly spaced ink strokes. The remaining blank paper leaves room for the future motion to be understood.
- A distinct page fold and a simple hand silhouette make the scene readable at native size. No tiny facial features, cup, screen, logos, or readable words.

**Palette:** deep indigo and muted brown shadows, soft amber light, warm cream paper, dark ink. Keep large shapes and strong value contrast so the lamp and notebook read at 64×64 without zooming.
