# The Light on the Page

**Status:** text concept only. No image has been generated or approved.

## Core scene

A quiet, cozy, lo-fi engineering desk seen from almost directly above. An engineer sits at the bottom edge with a pencil poised over a large sheet of drafting paper. A small incandescent desk lamp turns on. In its warm pool of light, the engineer draws a simple diagram: a few bold lines, nodes, and one small graph. The sheet gradually fills through the person's visible work.

Argon is used inside incandescent bulbs to protect the filament. The glowing filament provides the light. The physical argon in the lamp creates a visual bridge to the name Gemini 4 Argon. The scene should first read as a person absorbed in engineering work, then reveal that connection at the end.

## Proposed first static 64×64 frame

- Fixed, nearly overhead composition. One large sheet of drafting paper fills the center and right side; the lamp sits near the upper left; the engineer's head and shoulders enter from the bottom.
- The lamp has just turned on. A clear amber pool of light makes the paper the brightest, largest shape. The surrounding desk stays dark and calm.
- The paper is still mostly blank. One hand holds a pencil with its tip touching the sheet; one short line and two nodes hint at the diagram about to grow.
- A simple ruler or set square beside the sheet reinforces the engineering setting. Keep the person, pencil, and paper as distinct shapes at native size. No tiny facial features or readable writing.

**Palette:** deep indigo and muted brown shadows, soft amber light, warm cream paper, dark graphite lines. Keep large shapes and strong value contrast so the person, lamp, and drafting sheet read at 64×64 without zooming.
