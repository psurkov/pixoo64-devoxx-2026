A 2D game scene: pixel art made for a 64x64 LED display.

A cozy desk at night. A young woman sits at the left in a burgundy sweater, her face in profile and
lit warmly, a pencil hovering over a small completely blank cream sheet. A big glowing amber bulb on
a short round base stands on the right half of the desk and fills about a quarter of the frame
height; the dark window sits directly behind it so its round glass has a clear silhouette. A
sleeping orange cat curls along the top edge. A small mug sits farther back beside the lamp. The
window is flat deep navy with three plain building silhouettes and four amber window squares.

Draw this on a 64x64 grid using large square pixel blocks, each about one sixty-fourth of the image
wide. Six shapes carry the picture - woman, sheet, bulb, cat, mug, window - and everything else
stays dark and quiet. Separate every shape by lightness rather than by an outline. The bulb is the
brightest thing in the frame and a visible filament line inside it is welcome. Rich warm colors
against the blue night.

The attached image is a layout and color reference only. Keep its positions and its palette, but
draw far fewer and much larger shapes. No text, no logo, no star, no second light, no fine texture,
no hair strands, no wood grain, no anti-aliased edges, no photorealism, no watermark.
